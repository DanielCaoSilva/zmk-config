# KeyType

## Objective

KeyType provides an Open-Source directive service for input devices and microcontroller projects, meaning we facilitate and educate users, creators and subscribers to fufill thier opensource projects and ideas by sourcing materials and fabricators on (relatively) small orders.

### Services

- Sourcing and shipping of parts and fabrication
- Assembling and soldering services
- Firmware flashing and customizations
- Educational and documentation resources

### Creators

- Validated creators can upload and distribute their projects and receive a 50% profit gains in perpetuity

### Subscribers

- Receive updated products and kits as they are released
- Vote on the next supported project
